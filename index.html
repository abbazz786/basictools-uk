UK Self-Employed Tax Calculator

Calculate your Income Tax and National Insurance as a self-employed worker in England, Wales, or Northern Ireland. Covers the 2025/26 and 2026/27 tax years.

  • Personal Allowance: £12,570 (tapers above £100,000 income)
  • Basic rate: 20% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270
  • Higher rate: 40% on profits between £50,270 and £125,140
  • Additional rate: 45% on profits above £125,140
  • Class 4 NIC: 6% (main rate), 2% (above £50,270)
  • Class 2 NIC (voluntary): £3.50/week (2025/26), £3.65/week (2026/27)

Source: GOV.UK Income Tax rates

UK National Minimum Wage Checker

Check whether you are being paid at least the legal minimum wage. Enter your age, hours, and pay to get an instant result.

UK Minimum Wage Rates from April 2026

  • Age 21 and over (National Living Wage): £12.71 per hour
  • Age 18 to 20: £10.85 per hour
  • Age 16 to 17: £8.00 per hour
  • Apprentice rate: £8.00 per hour

If you are being underpaid, contact ACAS on 0300 123 1100 (free) or report it to HMRC. It is illegal to dismiss you for raising a minimum wage complaint.

Source: GOV.UK National Minimum Wage rates
